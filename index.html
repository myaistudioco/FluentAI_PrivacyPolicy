FluentAI — Learn English Speaking

📋 Introduction

Welcome to FluentAI — Learn English Speaking, developed by Secret Glow.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application. By using FluentAI, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

Last Updated: March 2026

📊 Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Usage Data: How you interact with the app — which tools you use, session duration, and feature usage patterns.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.
  • Voice Data: When you use the Pronunciation Coach feature, your voice input is processed in real-time for scoring. We do not store your voice recordings.
  • Purchase Information: If you subscribe to FluentAI Pro, purchase information is handled securely by Google Play Billing. We do not store your payment details.
  • Local Preferences: Your app settings such as dark/light mode, streak count, XP points, and daily usage are stored locally on your device using Android DataStore.

🤖 AI and Backend Services

FluentAI uses Anthropic Claude AI to power its 8 learning tools including AI Tutor Chat, Grammar Fixer, and Hindi to English Translator.

When you use these tools, your text input is sent securely to our backend server hosted on Vercel and processed by the AI. We do not store your conversation history on our servers.

All API communications are encrypted using HTTPS.

📱 Advertising

FluentAI uses Google AdMob to display advertisements to free users. AdMob may collect and use data to show you relevant ads.

This includes:

  • Advertising ID from your device
  • General location information
  • App usage information

Pro subscribers see zero advertisements anywhere in the app.

For more information about how Google uses data, visit: Google Privacy Policy

🔐 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and improve the FluentAI learning experience
  • Process your subscription and restore purchases
  • Display relevant advertisements to free users
  • Analyze app performance and fix bugs
  • Maintain your learning streak and XP progress locally

🔒 Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously:

  • All network communications use HTTPS encryption
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties
  • We do not store voice recordings
  • We do not store conversation history on our servers
  • Purchase processing is handled entirely by Google Play

👶 Children's Privacy

FluentAI is designed for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

🌍 Third Party Services

FluentAI integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — Advertising
  • Google Play Billing — Subscription management
  • Google Speech Recognition — Pronunciation scoring
  • Anthropic Claude AI — AI powered learning tools
  • Vercel — Backend API hosting

Each of these services has their own privacy policies which govern their use of your data.

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings
  • Cancel your Pro subscription at any time through Google Play
